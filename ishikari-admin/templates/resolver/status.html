About the resolver

The Dependency Resolver is a background service that manages job dependencies:

  • Monitors completed jobs and marks their dependencies as satisfied
  • Releases jobs to available state when all dependencies are satisfied
  • Handles failed jobs by marking their dependencies as failed
  • Cancels jobs with failed dependencies
  • Manages saga compensation when forward jobs fail

The resolver runs at a configurable interval (typically every second).
